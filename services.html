Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are completed in 2-4 weeks depending on complexity. Simple 5-page sites can be done in 2 weeks, while e-commerce or custom features may take 4-6 weeks.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans. Typically 50% upfront and 50% upon completion, or monthly payment options for larger projects.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely! All our websites are built mobile-first and fully responsive across all devices - phones, tablets, and desktops.

Can I update the website myself after it's built?

Yes! We use WordPress with Elementor, making it easy for you to update content, images, and pages. We also provide training.

What if I need changes after the website is launched?

We offer 1 month of free support with all packages. After that, you can choose a maintenance plan or pay hourly for updates.

Do you provide hosting?

We can recommend reliable hosting providers or manage hosting for you as part of our maintenance packages.

How much does SEO cost?

SEO pricing starts at $500/month for ongoing optimization or $1,200 for one-time setup. We customize packages based on your goals.

Can you help with content writing?

Yes! We can write all website content or work with your existing content. Content writing is available as an add-on service.

Do you work with businesses outside these 5 industries?

While we specialize in these 5 niches, we work with various industries. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

What's included in maintenance packages?

Maintenance includes updates, backups, security monitoring, performance optimization, and technical support. Details vary by plan tier.
