Our Story

Flexible Remote Services was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to provide exceptional web development and SEO services to small businesses that need them most. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a dedicated team of specialists serving clients across five key industries.

We noticed a gap in the market – event planners, medical professionals, electricians, plumbers, and jewelry businesses were being underserved by generic web agencies. They needed someone who understood their unique challenges, their customers, and their goals.

Over the past five years, we've built over 100 websites, helped businesses rank #1 in their local markets, and generated thousands of leads for our clients. But what we're most proud of is the relationships we've built and the success stories we've been part of.

Today, we continue to evolve, learn, and grow – always putting our clients' success first.
